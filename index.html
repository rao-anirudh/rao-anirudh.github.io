Anirudh Rao

I am a first-year DPhil student in Engineering Biology at the University of Oxford. I recently graduated from the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Madras with a BTech in Biological Engineering and an MTech in Data Science. I am interested in research at the intersection of synthetic biology, computational biology, and machine learning. I am driven by the belief that treating biology as an engineering discipline can unlock solutions to the world's most pressing problems.
